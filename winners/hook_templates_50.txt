# 50 Psychology-Based Hook Templates

### Use these when your **idea is clear**, but your **first line isn’t strong enough to stop the scroll**.

#### Pick a template. Replace the brackets. Keep it specific.

**#1 Template Hook:**
It took me [X years/months/reps] to learn [specific skill/lesson], but here’s the [short version/core lesson] in [short timeframe].
**Example:** “It took me 7 years to understand color grading, but here’s the 30-second version for beginners.”
Visual suggestion: Show old work vs current work side by side.

**#2 Template Hook:**
I used to [common wrong action]. Now I [contrarian/new action]. Here’s what changed.
**Example:** “I used to post more to grow my page. Now I delete half my ideas before filming. Here’s what changed.”

**#3 Template Hook:**
Most people think [common belief] is why [problem happens]. The real reason is usually [overlooked cause].
**Example:** “Most people think their skincare routine fails because of the products. The real reason is usually the order.”

**#4 Template Hook:**
To [desired outcome], stop doing [common method] and start doing [counterintuitive method] instead.
**Example:** “To get better at Spanish, stop memorizing random words and start repeating full sentences instead.”

**#5 Template Hook:**
I tested [method A] against [method B] for [time/reps]. The winner was not the one I expected.
**Example:** “I tested morning workouts against evening workouts for 30 days. The winner was not the one I expected.”
Visual suggestion: Show a simple A vs B split screen with the two methods.

**#6 Template Hook:**
[Number] mistakes that make [target audience] [undesired outcome], even when they’re doing [common good thing].
**Example:** “5 mistakes that make beginner photographers take flat photos, even when they’re using a good camera.”

**#7 Template Hook:**
This is the difference between [surface-level thing] and [thing that actually creates the result].
**Example:** “This is the difference between writing a caption and writing a caption that gets people to DM you.”
Visual suggestion: Show two examples on screen and point to the difference.

**#8 Template Hook:**
[Relatable person] got [dream outcome] with [simple/low-resource condition]. Here’s the method.
**Example:** “This student learned conversational French with 20 minutes a day. Here’s the method.”

**#9 Template Hook:**
Can you [achieve dream outcome] without [common constraint]? I tested [method] for [timeframe].
**Example:** “Can you build muscle without eating six meals a day? I tested a simpler plan for 60 days.”

**#10 Template Hook:**
Imagine [specific desired scene]. That starts with [vehicle/method].
**Example:** “Imagine opening your laptop on Monday and already having the week’s content planned. That starts with one content map.”
Visual suggestion: Show the desired scene first, then the tool/system behind it.
